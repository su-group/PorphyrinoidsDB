## 1. 术语定义（英文，可直接放进正文首次出现处）

> **macrocyclic scaffold** — the porphyrinoid ring system (the conjugated macrocycle together with its peripheral ring modifications and the central metalation state), indexed A01–A18 in this work.
>
> **meso-substituent** — the substituent at the four meso positions, grouped as B00–B15 in 16 substitution patterns.

首次出现后，正文可用简称 `scaffold` / `substituent`，但不得再引入 `macrocyclic topology`、`molecular backbone`、`side group` 等同义写法。
